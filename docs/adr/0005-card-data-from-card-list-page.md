# Card data and card look come from the fan-made card list

The printed card data (name, number, cost, Tags, Requirements, Victory Points, text) is extracted from the HTML of the fan-made card list at ssimeonoff.github.io/cards-list into our own card file. It is then checked against the open-source game's card definitions to catch mistakes. Card effects are authored by us (see ADR 0009), never derived from this data.

In the GUI, cards copy that page's layout and icon images. There are no card illustrations, because no clean source for them exists.
